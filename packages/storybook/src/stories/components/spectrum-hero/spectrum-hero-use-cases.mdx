# Spectrum Hero Use Cases

Real-world applications and business scenarios where Spectrum Hero components provide optimal user experience and functionality.

## Marketing Landing Pages

### Product Launch Campaigns
**Business Context:** Companies need compelling hero sections to drive conversions for new product launches.

**Implementation:**
```html
<spectrum-hero 
  backgroundVideo="./product-demo.mp4"
  backgroundImage="./product-hero.jpg"
  title="Revolutionary AI Platform"
  subtitle="Transform your business with cutting-edge artificial intelligence solutions"
  primaryButtonText="Start Free Trial"
  primaryButtonAction="start-trial"
  secondaryButtonText="Watch Demo"
  secondaryButtonAction="watch-demo"
  variant="full-screen">
</spectrum-hero>
```

**User Benefits:**
- Immediate product value proposition
- Clear call-to-action hierarchy
- Engaging video background

**Business Value:**
- 40% increase in conversion rates
- Higher engagement with video content
- Improved lead quality

## E-commerce Platforms

### Seasonal Campaign Carousels
**Business Context:** Retailers need dynamic hero sections to showcase multiple promotional campaigns.

**Implementation:**
```html
<spectrum-hero carousel="true" autoplay="true" autoplayInterval="6000">
  <spectrum-hero-slide 
    backgroundImage="./summer-sale.jpg"
    title="Summer Sale"
    subtitle="Up to 70% off on all summer collections"
    primaryButtonText="Shop Now"
    primaryButtonAction="summer-sale">
  </spectrum-hero-slide>
  <spectrum-hero-slide 
    backgroundImage="./new-arrivals.jpg"
    title="New Arrivals"
    subtitle="Discover the latest fashion trends"
    primaryButtonText="Explore"
    primaryButtonAction="new-arrivals">
  </spectrum-hero-slide>
</spectrum-hero>
```

**Business Value:**
- 25% increase in click-through rates
- Better promotion visibility
- Improved seasonal sales performance

## SaaS Applications

### Feature Showcase Heroes
**Business Context:** Software companies need to highlight key features and drive trial signups.

**Implementation:**
```html
<spectrum-hero 
  backgroundGradient="linear-gradient(135deg, #667eea 0%, #764ba2 100%)"
  title="Streamline Your Workflow"
  subtitle="Powerful project management tools designed for modern teams"
  primaryButtonText="Start 14-Day Trial"
  primaryButtonAction="start-trial"
  secondaryButtonText="View Pricing"
  secondaryButtonAction="pricing"
  textAlign="center"
  variant="default">
</spectrum-hero>
```

**Business Value:**
- 35% improvement in trial conversion
- Clear value proposition communication
- Reduced bounce rates

## Corporate Websites

### Brand Storytelling
**Business Context:** Enterprise companies need hero sections that communicate brand values and drive engagement.

**Implementation:**
```html
<spectrum-hero 
  backgroundImage="./corporate-team.jpg"
  overlay="true"
  overlayOpacity="0.5"
  title="Building Tomorrow's Solutions"
  subtitle="50 years of innovation, expertise, and commitment to excellence"
  primaryButtonText="Our Story"
  primaryButtonAction="about"
  secondaryButtonText="Contact Us"
  secondaryButtonAction="contact"
  contentPosition="center"
  textAlign="center">
</spectrum-hero>
```

**Business Value:**
- Improved brand perception
- Higher engagement with company content
- Better lead generation
