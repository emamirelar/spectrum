# Cookie Compliance Use Cases

Real-world applications and business scenarios where GDPR-compliant cookie consent management provides legal compliance, user trust, and optimized analytics data collection.

## Business Applications

### GDPR Compliance for E-commerce
**Business Context:** E-commerce websites must comply with GDPR regulations while maintaining effective analytics tracking for business intelligence. The cookie compliance component enables legal compliance while preserving the ability to collect consented analytics data.

**Implementation:**
```html
<!-- E-commerce GDPR compliance -->
<div class="ecommerce-compliance">
  <spectrum-cookie-compliance
    config='{
      "gtmContainerId": "GTM-ECOMMERCE",
      "consentVersion": "1.0.0",
      "cookieExpireDays": 365
    }'
    message="We use cookies to improve your shopping experience and analyze site performance."
    showDetails="true"
    position="bottom"
  >
  </spectrum-cookie-compliance>
</div>
```

**User Benefits:**
- Clear understanding of data collection practices
- Granular control over cookie preferences
- Trust through transparent privacy practices
- Seamless shopping experience with consented tracking

**Business Value:**
- Legal compliance with EU GDPR regulations
- Maintained analytics data quality through informed consent
- Reduced legal risk and potential fines
- Enhanced customer trust and brand reputation
