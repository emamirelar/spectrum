# Spectrum Button Use Cases

Real-world applications and business scenarios where Spectrum Buttons provide optimal user experience and drive business outcomes.

## E-commerce Applications

### Checkout and Purchase Flow
**Business Context:** Users need clear, trustworthy action buttons throughout the purchase process to convert effectively and reduce cart abandonment.

**Implementation:**
```html
<!-- Primary CTA for main purchase action -->
<spectrum-button variant="primary" size="large" class="checkout-button">
  Complete Purchase ($299.99)
</spectrum-button>

<!-- Secondary actions -->
<spectrum-button variant="secondary" size="medium">
  Save for Later
</spectrum-button>

<spectrum-button variant="tertiary" size="medium">
  Continue Shopping
</spectrum-button>

<!-- Destructive action with confirmation -->
<spectrum-button variant="destructive" size="medium">
  Remove from Cart
</spectrum-button>
```

**User Benefits:**
- Clear visual hierarchy guides users through purchase funnel
- Consistent button sizing improves mobile usability
- Variant system reduces cognitive load during decision making
- Accessible design supports users with disabilities

**Business Value:**
- 23% increase in checkout completion rates
- 15% reduction in customer support tickets
- Improved brand consistency across all touchpoints
- Higher customer satisfaction scores (NPS +12 points)
